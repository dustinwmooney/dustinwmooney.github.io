Let me save you some time.

If you’re looking for a polished personal brand with a color-coded content calendar and a headshot taken in a rented WeWork? Keep scrolling. This isn’t that.

This is my corner of the internet. The place where I keep my work, my writing, and whatever I’m currently chewing on at 6 AM with a cup of coffee and too many browser tabs open. I’ve always had opinions and I’ve always been building something. Now they live at the same address.

It’s built on one idea: a person’s website should sound like the person. No agency copy. No corporate sponsor whispering in my ear. Just a guy with a laptop, a resume, and a few projects he can’t stop tinkering with.

So what exactly are you looking at?

Glad you asked.

## What You’re Getting Into

**The work.** Fifteen years of customer success and strategy across SaaS, publishing, government, and automotive. I’ve managed millions in campaigns, led teams, kept clients who had every reason to leave, and learned that most business problems are people problems wearing a spreadsheet as a disguise. The resume is here, the PDF is one click away, and it’s always current. No graduate degree in doublespeak required to read it.

**The building.** I run a self-hosted AI assistant that handles my email, my calendar, my research, and my phone from a server I control. I’m building an app that lets regular people use open AI models without a subscription or a message cap. Neither of these came from a product roadmap. They came from being annoyed enough to fix it myself. I write about what actually works, what broke at 2 AM, and what I’d do differently.

**The thinking.** A running log of what I’m reading and wrestling with right now. Lately that’s the collision between technology and national security, and the question nobody in charge seems ready for: what happens to work when the machines get good at it. Short notes, dated, no filler. When one of them grows up, it becomes an essay.

**The writing.** Longer pieces, when a thought earns the space. Some are about strategy. Some are about the people who shaped me. All of them are mine, unedited by committee.

## So Why Stick Around?

Because you’re tired of websites that say nothing in a thousand words. You want someone who does the homework, brings the receipts, and still makes you laugh while the world burns around us. That’s the goal here. Sharp. Substantive. And yeah, a little sarcastic.

## Here’s the Deal

Everything on this site is free. There’s no paywall and no upsell hiding behind the third paragraph.

If you want to know when something new lands, drop your email in the box below. No schedule, no spam, and I’m not selling your address to anyone. If you’d rather just check back when you check back, that works too.

And if you’re here because you might want to work together? The resume is right there. The email is one click away. I answer it myself.

Welcome aboard. Buckle up.
